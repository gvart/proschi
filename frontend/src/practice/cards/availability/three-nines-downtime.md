## Question

A service promises 99.9% availability. How much downtime does that allow in a
30-day month?

## Solution

30 days × 24 h × 60 min = 43,200 minutes. 0.1% of that is **43.2 minutes**.
For reference: 99.99% allows about 4.3 minutes a month, 99% about 7.2 hours.

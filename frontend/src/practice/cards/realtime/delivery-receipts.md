## Text

Chat apps show "sent" when the {{server}} has stored a message, "delivered"
when it reached the recipient's {{device|phone|client}}, and "read" when the
recipient opened it.

## Why

Each tick is a separate small event flowing back to the sender, so receipts
multiply message traffic; large groups often aggregate or limit them.

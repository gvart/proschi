## Question

How much storage does one hour of video at 5 Mbit/s take?

## Solution

5 Mbit/s ÷ 8 = 0.625 MB/s. × 3,600 s = 2,250 MB ≈ **2.25 GB**. Video
services store several resolutions of each video, so the total is a few times
that.

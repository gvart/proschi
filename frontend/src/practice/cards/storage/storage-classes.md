## Front

Object storage offers hot, infrequent-access and archive classes. What do the
cheaper ones cost you?

## Back

They are cheaper **per GB stored** but charge **per retrieval**, often have a
minimum storage duration, and archive classes can take **minutes to hours**
to return data. Lifecycle rules move old objects down automatically, so
rarely watched old videos or logs cost less.

## Front

What is the quick rule for turning "per day" into "per second"?

## Back

A day has 86,400 seconds; round it to **100,000**. So 1 million a day is about
10 a second, and 1 billion a day about 10,000 a second.

## Why

Rounding to 10⁵ keeps the arithmetic in powers of ten and errs on the low side
by about 15%, which is well inside the accuracy an interview estimate needs.

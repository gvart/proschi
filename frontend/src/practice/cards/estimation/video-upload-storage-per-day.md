## Question

Users upload 500 hours of video every minute. After transcoding, all the
resolutions of one hour take about 1 GB in total. How much new storage is
needed per day?

## Solution

A day has 1,440 minutes: 500 × 1,440 = 720,000 hours of video a day.
× 1 GB = 720,000 GB = **720 TB a day**, before replication. That is about
260 PB a year, which is why cheaper storage tiers for old videos matter.

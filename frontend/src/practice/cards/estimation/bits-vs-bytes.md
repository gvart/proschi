## Text

Network links are quoted in {{bits|bit}} per second, while file sizes are in
bytes, so a 1 Gbit/s link moves at most about 125 MB/s: divide by {{8|eight}}.

## Why

Mixing up Mbit and MB is the most common bandwidth mistake in an estimate, and
it is off by a factor of eight.

## Text

A 30-day month has about {{2.6 million|2.5 million|2,600,000|2.6M}} seconds,
so 100 million requests a month average about {{40|38|39}} requests a second.

## Why

30 × 86,400 = 2,592,000 s. 100,000,000 ÷ 2,592,000 ≈ 38.6, so "about 40" is
the quick answer. Monthly figures are common in requirements (new URLs,
uploads), so this conversion comes up often.

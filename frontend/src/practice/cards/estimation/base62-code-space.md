## Question

A URL shortener uses 7-character codes made of a–z, A–Z and 0–9. How many
distinct codes are there?

## Solution

The alphabet has 26 + 26 + 10 = 62 symbols. 62⁷ = 62 × 62 × … seven times:
62² = 3,844; 62³ ≈ 238,000; 62⁴ ≈ 14.8 million; 62⁵ ≈ 916 million;
62⁶ ≈ 56.8 billion; 62⁷ ≈ **3.5 trillion**. At 100 million new links a month
that lasts thousands of years.

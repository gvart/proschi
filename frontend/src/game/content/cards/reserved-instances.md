## Text

App servers cost 30% less, but you cannot drop below today's app server replicas for 3 waves.

## Why

Committing to capacity for a year or three buys a large discount. The catch is the commitment: you pay for it whether you use it or not, so reserve the floor of your traffic and keep the peaks on demand.

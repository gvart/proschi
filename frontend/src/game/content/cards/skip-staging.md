## Text

$600 now: no staging environment to pay for. But bugs reach users first: 15 less Trust, and a lower cap.

## Why

A staging environment costs money and slows every release, which is why teams are tempted to drop it. The bugs it would have caught then land on real users, and every incident spends trust you have to earn back.

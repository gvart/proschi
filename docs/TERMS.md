# Terms

Short version: Proschi's code is open source under the MIT license, and the
editor, the simulation and the practice problems are free. These terms cover
the hosted service at [proschi.app](https://proschi.app): an account, the
Pro plan and interview packs. What is stored about you is on the
[Privacy](PRIVACY.md) page.

## The free service

You can use the editor and practice without an account. With one, the
account keeps 5 diagrams, 20 versions each, up to 64 KiB a diagram, and one
AI interview for life. The service is offered as is, without a warranty:
keep a copy of diagrams you can't lose (the editor's export, or the files in
your repository).

## Pro

Pro costs €12 a month or €89 a year, VAT included where it applies. It
gives:

- no practical limit on diagrams and versions in your account (a fair-use
  cap of 1000 of each, far beyond what anyone draws by hand), up to 512 KiB
  a diagram;
- 4 AI interviews a month, counted per calendar month (UTC); unused ones
  don't carry over;
- AI design review, interview history, an AI roadmap and private share
  links.

**Payment.** [Polar](https://polar.sh) is the merchant of record: you buy
from Polar, which charges your card, handles VAT and sends the invoices.
Polar's own terms apply to the payment. A subscription renews at the end of
each period until you cancel it.

**Canceling.** Cancel at any time in the billing portal (**Manage billing**
in Plan & billing on your account page). Pro lasts to the end of the period
you paid for, then the account moves to the free plan. Nothing is deleted:
every diagram and version is kept, and past the free plan's 5 diagrams the
ones saved least recently become read-only until you delete some or
subscribe again. Pack credits stay yours.

**Refunds.** Polar handles refund requests, by its refund policy and the
consumer law of the country you buy from. Ask through the billing portal or
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/gvart/proschi/issues).

## Interview packs

A pack is 5 AI interviews for €15, on any plan. Pack credits never expire
while the account exists. Pro's monthly interviews are used first, then the
free one, then pack credits. Interviews already used from a pack aren't
refundable: a refunded pack takes back only its unused credits.

## AI interviews

One credit is used when an interview starts. If an interview fails on our
side within its first 5 minutes, the credit is given back. An interview
lasts at most 60 minutes. The interviewer is an AI model: its questions,
hints and verdict are practice feedback, not an assessment of you by a
person, and can be wrong. What is sent to the AI provider and how long the
transcript is kept is on the [Privacy](PRIVACY.md#ai-interviews) page.

## Fair use

Don't use the service to store or share unlawful or abusive content, to
attack it, or to resell it. An account that does can be blocked, as
described on the [Privacy](PRIVACY.md#the-sites-operator) page.

## Deleting your account

**Delete account** on your account page cancels a Pro subscription at once
and deletes the account with its unused interview credits. Download your data first if you want a copy.

## Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters to a paying account, the change
is announced on the [blog](https://proschi.app/blog/) at least 30 days
before it applies to a renewal. Questions go to
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/gvart/proschi/issues).

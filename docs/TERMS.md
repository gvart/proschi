# Terms

Short version: Proschi's code is open source under the MIT license, and the
editor, the simulation and the practice problems are free. These terms cover
the hosted service at [proschi.app](https://proschi.app): an account, the
Pro and Max plans, credits and top-ups. What is stored about you is on the
[Privacy](PRIVACY.md) page.

## The free service

You can use the editor and practice without an account. With one, the
account keeps 5 diagrams, 20 versions each, up to 64 KiB a diagram, and
gets 100 credits once, at sign-up. The service is offered as is, without a
warranty: keep a copy of diagrams you can't lose (the editor's export, or
the files in your repository).

## Credits

Mock interviews and Design partner are paid for in credits. One credit is
worth €0.01. Each action is charged for what it used, so a short answer
costs less than a long one; it shows an estimate before it starts, and the
real cost is in **Plan & billing** on your account page afterwards. You
pay for what the model did: an answer it gave is charged even when it was
cut off, declined or came out malformed. An answer that fails on our side
(a network error, a timeout, a busy service) isn't charged. Monthly credits are spent
first, then the sign-up credits, then top-ups.

## Pro and Max

Pro costs €12 a month or €99 a year, and Max €29 a month or €249 a year,
VAT included where it applies. Both give:

- no practical limit on diagrams and versions in your account (a fair-use
  cap of 1000 of each, far beyond what anyone draws by hand), up to 512 KiB
  a diagram;
- credits every month: 800 on Pro, 2,200 on Max, counted per calendar month
  (UTC); unused monthly credits don't carry over;
- interview history and trends, your next steps and private share links.

Max adds Deep mode, which runs the verdict and Design partner on the
strongest model, charged at that model's price, and a printable report of
each verdict.

**Switching.** Switching between Pro and Max, or between monthly and yearly,
changes the subscription at once; Polar charges or settles the difference
for the rest of the period.

**Payment.** [Polar](https://polar.sh) is the merchant of record: you buy
from Polar, which charges your card, handles VAT and sends the invoices.
Polar's own terms apply to the payment. A subscription renews at the end of
each period until you cancel it.

**Canceling.** Cancel at any time in the billing portal (**Manage in Polar**
in Plan & billing on your account page). The plan lasts to the end of the
period you paid for, then the account moves to the free plan. Nothing is
deleted: every diagram and version is kept, and past the free plan's 5
diagrams the ones saved least recently become read-only until you delete
some or subscribe again. Top-up credits stay yours.

**Refunds.** Polar handles refund requests, by its refund policy and the
consumer law of the country you buy from. Ask through the billing portal or
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/gvart/proschi/issues).

## Top-ups

A top-up is 1,000 credits for €12, on any plan. Top-up credits never
expire while the account exists. Credits already spent from a top-up aren't
refundable: a refunded top-up takes back only its unused credits.

## Mock interviews

Starting an interview needs enough credits for a short session, and sets
aside the cost of its verdict. If the credits run out during the interview,
it moves to the wrap-up and still gives its verdict. An interview lasts at
most 60 minutes. The interviewer is an AI model: its questions,
hints and verdict are practice feedback, not an assessment of you by a
person, and can be wrong. What is sent to the AI provider and how long the
transcript is kept is on the [Privacy](PRIVACY.md#mock-interviews) page.

## Fair use

Don't use the service to store or share unlawful or abusive content, to
attack it, or to resell it. An account that does can be blocked, as
described on the [Privacy](PRIVACY.md#the-sites-operator) page.

## Deleting your account

**Delete account** on your account page cancels a Pro or Max subscription at
once and deletes the account with its unused credits. Download your data first if you want a copy.

## Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters to a paying account, the change
is announced on the [blog](https://proschi.app/blog/) at least 30 days
before it applies to a renewal. Questions go to
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/gvart/proschi/issues).
